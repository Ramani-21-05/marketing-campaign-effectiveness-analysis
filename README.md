# Marketing Campaign Effectiveness Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes the effectiveness of marketing campaigns across multiple digital channels using Power BI.  
The goal is to evaluate ROI, conversion efficiency, and marketing spend to support data-driven budget allocation decisions.

---

## Business Problem
The marketing team invests heavily in channels such as Facebook, Google Ads, YouTube, Email, and Websites.  
Despite increasing spend, management lacks clarity on:
- Which channels generate the highest ROI
- Which campaigns underperform
- How efficiently marketing budgets are utilized

---

## 📈 Key KPIs
- Total Marketing Spend
- Average ROI
- Average Conversion Rate
- Average / Max / Min Campaign Spend

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features
- Interactive slicers (Channel, Date)
- ROI & Conversion analysis by channel
- Spend distribution analysis
- Budget optimization scatter chart
- Campaign-level performance table

---

## 🔍 Analysis Performed
- Data quality checks and cleaning
- Univariate analysis on marketing spend
- Channel-wise performance comparison
- Identification of underperforming campaigns
- ROI-based budget optimization insights

---

## 💡 Key Insights
- High ROI channels identified for increased investment
- High-spend but low-ROI campaigns flagged for optimization
- Conversion efficiency varies significantly across channels

---

## 🚀 Business Recommendations
- Reallocate budget towards high-ROI channels
- Reduce spend on consistently underperforming campaigns
- Continuously monitor campaign ROI using interactive dashboards
